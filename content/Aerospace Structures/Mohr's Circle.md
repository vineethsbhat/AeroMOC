|**Goal**|**Stress Formula**|**Strain Formula**|
|---|---|---|
|**Average/Center**|$\frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2}$|$\frac{\epsilon_x + \epsilon_y}{2}$|
|**Max "Radius"**|$\sqrt{(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2})^2 + \tau_{xy}^2}$|$\sqrt{(\frac{\epsilon_x - \epsilon_y}{2})^2 + (\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{2})^2}$|
|**Principal Values**|$\sigma_{avg} \pm R$|$\epsilon_{avg} \pm R_{strain}$|
