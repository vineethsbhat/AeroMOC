1. In the 1970s there was a need to improve respiratory protection for firefighters which eventually led to widespread use of COPVs
2. Initially they were all made of conventional steel or aluminium 
3. They were designed to operate at 2,200 psi. The physical dimensions was still a limitation, they were bulky. 
4. In space applications Kevlar COPVs became widely used for high pressure helium and nitrogen storage, especially on the space shuttle orbiter
5. Early during design focus was on the liner and little emphasis on overwrap.
