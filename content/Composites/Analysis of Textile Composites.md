### Non Crimp Fabrics :
1. Several UD-layers of rovings' are stacked upon each other and hold together by stiches
2. Fibers are overlapped and fixed by stitching
3. Fibers remain substantially straight
4. CLT is applicable 

### Woven Fabrics :
1. Two fiber directions (0 and 90)
2. The fiber's now have undulations since one goes over the other
3. CLT cannot be used directly and a modification has to be done to account for the undulations
4. Plain Weave, Twill Weave and Satin Weave
