The most commonly used criteria are :
1. Damage tolerant criteria where the $\epsilon_1. \epsilon_2 < 0.4 \%$ for aerospace structures
2. Fatigue criteria where the $\epsilon_1. \epsilon_2 < 0.2 \%$
3. Combination of Tsai-Wu and Max. stress 
4. Partly interactive criteria are currently mainly used for academic calculations. Puck’s criterion is currently starting to get into industrial application

==The choice of a failure criterion depends on experience (e.g. with the criterion) and available input parameters.==
