Composites (FRP) are generally elastic and brittle in nature, while metals used are elastic-plastic and ductile in nature. 
The curve of specific strength vs. specific stiffness shows unidirectional carbon fiber/epoxy (High Tenacity and High Modulus) material has incredibly high values for both. Materials such as steel, aluminium have very low specific strength and specific stiffness.

[Rule of Thirds]
Aluminium :
	Density = 2.5 g/cm3
	Stiffness = 70 GPa
Titanium 
	Density = 4.7 g/cm3
	Stiffness = 112 GPa
Steel 
	Density = 7.8 g/cm3
	Stiffness = 210 GPa

[Where does carbon fiber/composite fit in this ?]
Carbon fiber has a density of 1.5 g/cm3 and a stiffness of 230 GPa
