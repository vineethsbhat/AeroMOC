1. Burst from Over-pressurisation : The unexpected burst of a COPV from over-pressurisation can be limited from material certification, design for conservative material allowable, and [proof pressurisation] during acceptance testing. The external pressurisation must also be controlled.
2. Fatigue Failure of Liner : It can be mitigated by inspection and testing. NDI of liner to make sure flaw size is not above the limit. Then perform and pressurisation test above the design life and ensure you have enough margin on cycle life
3. Burst from Liner or Composite Damage : This can be prevented by having damage tolerant design strategies and testing. Visual inspection to identify surface damage or flaws
4. Stress Rupture of Composite Overwrap : Fiber wrapped pressure vessels experience something known as [stress rupture/static fatigue]. It is a phenomena where the composite experiences degradation as a function of time. This results in a sudden failure of the overwrap resulting in rapid expansion of the stored contents leading to damage of the surrounding. 
5. Buckling of Liners : (during depressurization, purge, vacuum/autoclave cure, etc.)
