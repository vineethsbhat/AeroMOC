>What are composite materials ?

Generally speaking composite materials are those materials that are made up of one or more constituent materials. In majority of engineering applications we use materials that are homogenous (one material throughout the body) and isotropic (material property is uniform throughout the body). 

Composites violate this rule and are non-homogenous (two or more materials) and anisotropic (properties varying with directions inside the body). They do so by having fibers and matrix. Matrix takes the role of a fixing binder in which fibers are embedded. 

>What is the function of fibers and matrix ?

Fibers have high stiffness, high strength, good fatigue characteristic, and they act as the primary load carrying component in a composite

Whereas, matrix is responsible for fixing the fiber in desired position, protect the fibers from the environment, it transmits the forces to the fibers and acts as an interface for load transfer between fibers.
