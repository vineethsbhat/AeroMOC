>[!Important]
>1. Coupling always appears if $\mu_{12}  \neq 0$
>2. For non-symmetric laminates
>3. If $\alpha \neq 0$ and $\alpha \neq 90$ and unbalanced

>[!Note]
>1. For mid-plane symmetry $[B] = 0$ - no extension bending coupling
>2. For balanced laminate (equal alpha and -alpha plies), $A_{16}$ and $A_{26}$ $= 0$, therefore no extension-shear coupling
>3. For length plane symmetry (only 0 and 90 plies) - no extension shear and bending torsion coupling, thus $A_{16}, A_{26}, D_{16}, D_{26} = 0$
