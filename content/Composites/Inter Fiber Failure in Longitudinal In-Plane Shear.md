For shear loading there are always pairwise connected stresses with perpendicular action planes. You can have two fracture planes. It is all about shear action happening along the length of the laminate. Fracture occurs on the plane that has lower fracture resistance. Plane generally appears parallel to fibers

At first micro cracks appear at $45 \degree$ as principle stress cracks between the fibers. Eventually the crack forms parallel to fiber direction and in this case, fibers are acting as crack arrestors.
