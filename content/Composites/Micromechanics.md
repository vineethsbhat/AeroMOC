>"Estimation of the relation between the (mechanical) properties of fibers and matrix and the macroscopic, homogenized and averaged properties of a lamina.”

Fiber Stiffness = $E_f$ 
Fiber Strength = $X_f$
Matrix Stiffness = $E_m$
Matrix Strength = $X_m$

==The goal is to predict the stiffness of the lamina and understand the influence of fibers and matrix properties on lamina behaviour==

## The concept of Fiber Volume Fraction (FVF)
$$\phi_f = \frac{V_f}{V_c}$$
$$\phi_f + \phi_m = 1$$ provided the voids = 0, $\phi_v = 0$
Average composite density is given as, $$\rho_c = \rho_f \cdot \phi_f + \rho_m \cdot \phi_m$$
- [k] Airbus standard: 2.5% void content allowable); this is neglected in micromechanics. In the real world the $\phi_v$ always exists
==Repeating Unit Cell== : smallest possible cell element, which represents the pattern of a periodic material microstructure.
==RVE== : contains large enough volume to capture the random microstructure on a statistical basis

>The geometric max. for FVF is around 90,7 % while typical FVFs for prepegs and NCFs are 50-60%

At the lamina level assuming plane stress condition we need, $E1,E2, G12, \mu_{12}$
