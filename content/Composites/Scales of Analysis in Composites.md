Since we have a non-homogenous material, the material response is quite complex. To allow for the use of mechanical definition of stresses, strains and macroscopic approach is applied. A UD lamina is considered a homogenous continuum (==smeared approach==) 

There are two scales to look at :

[[Micromechanics]] : Study of fibers, matrix and the interaction between them. Heterogenous behaviour since fiber and matrix are two distinct materials at this level. 

[[Macromechanics]] : Analysing the material at the (lamina and laminate) one or several ply levels.

- [k] Note that stiffness property becomes homogenized as we go from micro level to macro level but failure is localised as we go from macro to micro level.
