1. Inter-fiber failure starting in one ply can be stopped by adjacent plies with different fiber orientation since fibers act as crack arrestor. An even distribution of ply angles over the laminate thickness should be accomplished
2. [Design rule: maximum two plies with same orientation next to each other]
3. Inter-fiber failure may however lead to catastrophic failure of the laminate for the case of wedge fracture under transverse compression dominated loading.
