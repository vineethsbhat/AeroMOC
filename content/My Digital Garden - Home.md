# Welcome!

I'm a master's graduate from Technical University of Munich, with a focus on structural analysis, composite structure design and process simulation. This site is my attempt to make a living knowledge base that connects various topics across the three domains. 

You'll find notes which I hope are intuitive by nature and link between various ideas we encounter. 

Here's how you can explore :
