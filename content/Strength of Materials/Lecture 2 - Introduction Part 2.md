### Failure of Brittle Materials Subjected to Tension or Torsion :

When a chalk is subjected to tension and torsional force, we see the way in which the chalk breaks. Something happens to the material on the respective planes which reaches a critical value and the material separates. We know the chalk resists the applied load, so we must bring in the plane passing through the points at the point of separation

>So what exactly is happening ?

Consider a tensile test for a mild steel specimen. When you look at the load vs displacement curve and convert it to a stress-strain curve, we see a non-linear relationship. We see a elastic region and a plastic region. When we look at the failure of ductile materials, it is visibly distinct, it fails by ==cup and cone fracture==

The advantage of idealizing in terms of small deformations is writing the equations for undeformed configurations as well as working within the linear region. 

The combined effect of various loading conditions within the linear region is studied using the 'Principle of Superposition'

## Rapid Prototyping - Additive Manufacturing

Model Fabrication in Stereolithography :

A beam of UV light is focused on the top layer of liquid photo curable resin. Earlier systems used He-Cd laser which are now replaced by Argon-Ion lasers. The beams are are positioned and moved in x and y directions using a set of mirrors to crosshatch the area.

Curing is done over the depth of the layer in a parabolic fashion due to the resin obeying the Beer-Lambert Law. This leads to pockets of uncured resin within the model. This trapped resin is cured during post-processing and the model becomes elastically inhomogeneous.
