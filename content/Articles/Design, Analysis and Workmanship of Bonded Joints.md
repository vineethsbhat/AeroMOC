#### Why go for bonded joints ?
- There are a few reasons why we go for adhesively bonded joints
	- They are lightweight
	- Low stress concentration in adherends
	- Excellent fatigue life
	- Damage tolerance

>They do have issues with inspection, environmental degradation, sensitive to peel and cleavage stresses, cannot be disassembled, high level of quality control needed
#### Why go for bolted joints ?

- Easy to disassemble
- No thickness limitation
- Manufacturing is straightforward
- Insensitive to peel forces

>But they do have higher stress concentration, added weight due to fasteners, prone to fatigue, fretting problems and extensive shimming is needed 

>[!Important]
>But generally speaking we go for bonded joints because it has improved product performance, reduces vibration and has great damping properties
